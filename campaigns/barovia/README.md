### Avatar Crops

`visual-drafts/` also holds 512×512 square crops of both treatments, suffixed `-avatar.png`. These
exist because Discord proxy bots such as Tupperbox render a character's image as a small circle, and
the full portraits are 2:3 with the face in the upper third — a naive square crop cuts the head off.
The crops are centred on the face and keep the spectacles, the violet tear-track and the choker
inside the circular mask.

They are served directly from this repository over `raw.githubusercontent.com`, which is a more
durable avatar host than a Discord CDN link since those are signed and expire.

## About The Character

Marcel is a changeling bard of the College of Creation who does not speak. He communicates through
mime, staged tableaux, music, and prepared Magic Mouth stones. Because he has no voice, what he
chooses to look like functions as his opening line — which makes him an unusually useful subject
for visual-continuity work, since the costume is load-bearing rather than decorative.

The Mourner is the face he wears for a mission investigating disappearances in the town of Vallaki.
